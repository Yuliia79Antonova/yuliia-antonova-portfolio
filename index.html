About Me

Passionate about bringing characters to life

I'm a 3rd year student at South East Technological University (SETU), specializing in Computing in Interactive Digital Art and Design. My passion lies in creating memorable characters that tell stories and evoke emotions.

From hand-drawn turnarounds to pixel art sprites and game UI design, I love exploring different styles and techniques. I'm particularly drawn to the playful, educational animation style that studios like Brown Bag Films excel at.
